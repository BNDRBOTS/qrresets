Protocol.

01

Distribute

The current phase builds the payment infrastructure and landing interface ensuring each dollar moves cleanly, establishing the first QR codes across Metro, Sedona, and other strategic locations. Quiet outreach forms an integral part of this foundation, connecting support networks with individuals efficiently.

02

Scan & Route

Donors contribute to a dignity-first support flow: a scanned code channels a dollar directly toward a real person’s urgent request; QuickResets maintains the process thoughtfully, keeping interactions reviewed and traceable to safeguard both donors and recipients while ensuring absolute privacy.

03

Review & Restore

Requests are processed with careful verification to protect the support pool, maintaining full focus on providing practical and respectful solutions.

When adjusting a request, the primary goal remains securing a respectful path that works — a refined version of the request, direct payment to the vendor, a coordinated ride, a trusted referral, or another practical route that safeguards everyone involved.

The One-Dollar Principle.

QuickResets delivers focused micro-support for critical moments — a single dollar from the donor side creates a tangible restoration on the recipient side, building a direct path toward stability precisely when it is most effective.

The $1 structure keeps giving straightforward: scanning a code allows individuals to join a clean line of micro-support for someone facing immediate hardship. Every dollar stands whole, ensuring donors give comfortably and recipients receive support respectfully, preserving their dignity.
